

## Update Favicon

Copy the uploaded logo to `public/favicon.png` and update `index.html` to reference it as the site favicon.

### Steps
1. Copy `user-uploads://Orange_Modern_Consulting_Logo.png` to `public/favicon.png`
2. Add `<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.png" type="image/png">` to the `<head>` in `index.html`
